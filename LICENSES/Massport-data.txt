Shuttle times: Massport.

The Logan Airport shuttles' times in Starchart's walker's timetable
(data/routing/greater-boston-transit-v1.bin.gz, built by
chart/transit/build_timetable.py --massport) are supplied by Massport (the
Massachusetts Port Authority), from its GTFS feed published by Trillium
Solutions:

  https://data.trilliumtransit.com/gtfs/massport-ma-us/massport-ma-us.zip

They are used and displayed under the Massport Basic Data Feed License
Agreement (https://www.massport.com/media/1651/massportdatalicenseagreement.pdf):
a revocable, royalty-free, non-exclusive licence. The content stays
Massport's; Starchart is not Massport, nor its agent or partner, and uses no
Massport logo. The feed is refreshed at each release, and times can be wrong.
