Bus stop icon: the MBTA's bus pictogram.

The bus stop icon Starchart shows with SCUL mode off (design/glyphs/bus_stop.svg,
drawn by design/glyphs/build.py, its MBTA_BUS path) carries the bus pictogram of
the MBTA's digital style guide (author: the MBTA, 2019), as published on
Wikimedia Commons:

  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Icon-mode-bus-default.svg

Commons tags it public domain in the United States (PD-MAGov): "This work is in
the public domain in the United States because it was a Commonwealth of
Massachusetts public record disseminated by a Commonwealth agency or the
Massachusetts Archives." It also carries a trademark note: the pictogram is a
mark of the MBTA, used here to mean a bus stop. Starchart is not connected with
or endorsed by the MBTA. The disc, its outline and their colours are
Starchart's art (CC BY-SA 4.0).
