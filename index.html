About›

A star chart for SCUL pilots, made for Cadet arclight's cadet trial.

Your location never leaves this droid.

No accounts, no analytics, no trackers.

The source: github.com/peterdsharpe/starchart.

Starchart

A star chart for SCUL pilots: a bike map and navigator that runs on your droid.

  • Search for a place to set a course, or tap Launch to just record the ride.
  • It speaks SCUL: a street is a starpath, a traffic light a pulsar, a bike path a wormhole, and a mile a light year. New to SCUL? Read the lingo at scul.org/lingo.
  • Your location never leaves this droid.
  • Add it to your home screen.
